# TELECONNECT ML ASSIGNMENT

## PROJECT DESCRIPTION

This project focuses on Customer Churn Prediction and Monthly Charges Prediction using Machine Learning techniques.

The project includes:

. Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)  
. Data Preprocessing & Feature Engineering  
. Classification Models  
. Regression Models  
. SHAP Interpretation & Business Recommendations  

## DATASET

. Telco Customer Churn Dataset  

## TECHNOLOGIES USED

. Python  
. Pandas  
. NumPy  
. Scikit-learn  
. Matplotlib  
. Seaborn  
. SHAP  

## CLASSIFICATION MODELS

. Logistic Regression  
. Decision Tree  
. Random Forest  
. SVM  
. KNN  

## REGRESSION MODELS

. Linear Regression  
. Ridge Regression  
. Lasso Regression  
. ElasticNet  
. Decision Tree Regressor  
. Random Forest Regressor  
. SVR  

## PROJECT GOAL

To identify customers likely to churn and provide business insights to improve customer retention and revenue.
